St. Joseph's College, Darjeeling · North Point

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) · affiliated to the University of North Bengal

A historic Jesuit institution founded in 1888 by Belgian Jesuit Fr. Henri Depelchin, with the college section beginning in 1927 at North Point, Singamari. Run by the Darjeeling Jesuits of North Bengal, it's a co-educational Catholic minority degree college serving ~3,000 students with undergraduate & postgraduate programs — living by its motto "Sursum Corda" — "Lift up your hearts."
